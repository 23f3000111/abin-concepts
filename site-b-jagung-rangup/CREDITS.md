# Credits and swap-outs: Concept B, Jagung Rangup

## Client material (ABIN Snack Food)

- Logo: cut from ABIN's artwork, upscaled 4x with Real-ESRGAN (anime model) on a transparent background. Replace it with the vector logo when the client sends it. The favicon comes from the same artwork.
- Packs (`assets/img/packs/`): the eight pack renders (four flavours, 30 g and 60 g) from the client's files, cut from their white backgrounds. The Variety 4-pack image is the four 30 g packs fanned together.
- Corn sticks (`assets/img/sticks/`): the single stick from page 2 of the 2024 catalogue, tinted per flavour.
- Ingredients (`assets/img/ingredients/`, `assets/img/orbs/`): chilli, cheese and corn cut from the 60 g pack renders; the nori piece is built from the Seaweed pack's nori texture. Corn kernels come from the same renders.
- Certification marks (`assets/img/certs/`): the halal mark from the pack, upscaled 4x; MeSTI and Produk Malaysia rendered from the catalogue's vector artwork.
- Photos (`assets/img/photo/`): the bowl photos from the catalogue's cover and back page, the team photo at the Rawang factory and four shelf photos from the client's files.
- Posters (`assets/img/posters/`): ABIN's own posters (four flavours in English, Malay and Chinese, the trade banner, It's 3PM, game night, affiliate, bold flavour).
- Video (`assets/video/`): ABIN's six video ads, re-encoded for the web with poster frames. The four flavour clips are cut from the flavour-by-flavour ad, and the stills in `assets/img/stills/` are frames from the ads.
- Downloads (`assets/docs/`): the 2024 product catalogue (recompressed) and ABIN's packaging specification sheet.
- Trade channel photos (`assets/img/channels/`): supermarket, convenience store and export from the client's shelf photos.
- Barcodes are drawn in the browser from the EAN-13 numbers on the packs and the catalogue.
