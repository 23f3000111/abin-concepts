How Kali Kali started

ABIN started with a simple idea: a corn snack that is properly crunchy and still easy to say yes to every day. Today our crew makes about 90 kg of Kali Kali a day. The recipe moved to gluten-free corn, every pack carries the JAKIM halal logo, and all four flavours are plant-based with no preservatives.
