A snack worth the crunch

ABIN started with a simple idea: a corn snack that is properly crunchy and still easy to say yes to every day.

Today our team makes about 90 kg of Kali Kali a day at our own factory in Rawang, roughly 1,500 packs. The recipe now uses gluten-free corn, every pack carries the JAKIM halal logo, and all four flavours are plant-based with no preservatives.

You will find us in supermarkets and corner shops in Malaysia, on Shopee, at food fairs from Kuala Lumpur to Tokyo, and in bulk orders heading to the UAE.

What we stand for

Four promises that shape every bag that leaves Rawang.

Plant-based by default

Every flavour starts with corn and plant ingredients. Original and Seaweed are vegan-friendly, and Spicy and Cheese suit vegetarians.

Crunch without compromise

No preservatives and 0% trans fat, and still the loudest crunch in the bowl. That is the whole point of Kali Kali.

Made for everyone

Halal, gluten-free and plant-based, so the whole table can share one bag: kids at recess, colleagues at 3 PM, friends on game night.

Let's grow together

Shops, distributors and affiliates are partners, not just buyers. We are a reliable supplier for local and international markets, and we want you to grow with us.

The people behind the crunch

Our crew mixes, twists, seasons and packs every Kali Kali at Kilang Makanan Ringan ABIN in Taman Perindustrian Belmas Johan, Rawang.
