Our six promises

Berasaskan tumbuhan

Plant-based

Kali Kali is made from corn and plant-based ingredients. Original and Seaweed are vegan-friendly, and Spicy and Cheese are suitable for vegetarians.

Bebas gluten

Gluten-free

ABIN moved the recipe to gluten-free corn so more people can enjoy it, and every pack is labelled gluten-free.

Tanpa pengawet

No preservatives

There are no added preservatives. The packs are sealed fresh instead, with an 18-month shelf life on the 30 g pack.

Lemak trans sifar

0% trans fat

Every pack is labelled 0% trans fat, and ABIN describes Kali Kali as made from corn, not deep-fried.

Jagung bukan GMO

Non-GMO corn

It all starts with non-GMO corn, the same promise ABIN prints on its packs and trade banners.

Halal diperakui

Halal certified

ABIN is halal-certified by JAKIM, Malaysia's halal authority. Look for MS1500 and the halal logo on every pack.
